# Marketplace Seller Policy Handbook (Excerpt)

Sample knowledge-base document — for SellerPulse RAG corpus. Not a real marketplace policy.

## 1. Listing Content Standards
<!-- id: pol-1 | topics: listings, content, images | type: prose -->

All product listings must accurately describe the item's condition, materials, dimensions, and
included accessories. Title and description must not include unrelated keywords added only to
influence search ranking.

Product images must depict the actual item being sold. Stock photos are permitted only when
clearly representative of the exact variant listed (color, size, model).

## 2. Review Guidelines
<!-- id: pol-2a | topics: reviews, incentives, solicitation | type: prose -->

Sellers may ask buyers to leave honest feedback about their order, but may not condition any
discount, refund, free item, or other incentive on leaving a positive review, or on leaving any
review at all.

Prohibited practices include:

- Offering a gift card, free product, or refund in exchange for a 5-star review.
- Asking a buyer to remove or change a negative review in exchange for compensation.
- Posting or soliciting reviews for products the reviewer never purchased.

Violations may result in listing suspension and are reported to the marketplace trust & safety
team.

## 2. Review Guidelines — Enforcement Tiers
<!-- id: pol-2b | topics: reviews, enforcement, penalties | type: table -->

A MINOR violation — for example, a friendly reminder email asking for honest feedback — results
in a warning notice on first offense, and a warning notice on repeat offense.

A MODERATE violation — for example, offering a discount code in exchange for a 5-star review —
results in a 7-day listing suspension on first offense, and a 30-day listing suspension on repeat
offense.

A SEVERE violation — for example, paying a third party to post fake reviews — results in
immediate listing removal on first offense, and account termination on repeat offense.

## 3. Inventory & Fulfillment
<!-- id: pol-3 | topics: inventory, fulfillment | type: prose -->

Listings must be marked out-of-stock or deactivated within 24 hours of inventory reaching zero.
Sellers may not continue accepting orders for items they cannot fulfill within the stated
delivery window.

## 4. Pricing
<!-- id: pol-4 | topics: pricing, discounts | type: prose -->

Sellers set their own prices but may not use deceptive strike-through ('was' price) claims unless
the item was actually offered at that price for a continuous prior period as defined in the full
pricing policy.

## 5. Communication with Buyers
<!-- id: pol-5 | topics: reviews, communication, promotional-links | type: prose -->

All buyer-facing messages, including review replies, must be professional and may not contain
promotional links unrelated to resolving the buyer's issue. Automated messages must be clearly
reviewed before sending; sellers remain responsible for their content.
